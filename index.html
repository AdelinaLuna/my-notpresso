Two Ways to Keep the Same Capsule on EN 350.G:

  1. In-Chamber Water-Pause Mode (Recommended — Never Drops Capsule): Starts a 130 ml brew. When Phase 1 (or your small water fill) finishes, lift the water tank slightly (~1 cm) or let it run out.   2. 2-Stage Automated Macro: Sends Phase 1 (40 ml / 15 ml), counts down 60s / 10s (letting you re-drop the cleaning capsule & flick the slider), then automatically fires Phase 2 (130 ml).
