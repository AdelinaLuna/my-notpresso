How to get your Auth Key:

  1. Enable Developer Options on Android.
  2. Turn on Enable Bluetooth HCI snoop log.
  3. Open the official Nespresso app (or trigger a brew once).
  4. Pull btsnoop_hci.log and inspect write requests to characteristic 06aa3a41.
  5. Copy the 16 hex characters (starts with 8) and paste above.
