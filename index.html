📄 pp. 2 & 4 — Specifications & Pairing

  • Electrical / Pressure: 220–240 V~, 50–60 Hz, 1150–1260 W • Pump max 19 bar (1.9 MPa).
  • Radio BLE: 2.402–2.480 GHz, max transmit power 4 dBm • Weight ~4.4 kg (9.7 lbs).
  • Dimensions & Tank: Tank ~1.1 L (37.5 oz) • 38.05 × 28.7 × 22.3 cm (15 × 11.3 × 8.8 in).
  • Pairing Complete: The Nespresso Light blinks 3 times to confirm pairing.

🎛️ p. 8 — Machine Overview & Controls

  • Top / Front LEDs: Maintenance Light (top right), Brewing Button, Nespresso Light (capsule stock management, top left).
  • Physical Dials: Temperature dial (0 / Warm, Hot, Extra Hot) and Beverage dial (Ristretto, Espresso, Lungo, Americano, Hot water).
  • Hardware Parts: Capsule insertion slider, coffee & hot water outlet, adjustable cup support & drip grid, drip tray, capsule container, 1.1 L tank & base.

☕ pp. 9–12 — ON/OFF, First Rinse & Coffee Preparation

  • Turn ON / Heat Up: Press Brew button, turn either dial, or open slider. Lights blink while heating (~25–30s); steady lights = ready.
  • Turn OFF: Turn Temperature dial anti-clockwise all the way to 0 (light blinks 5 seconds before shutoff).
  • First-Use Rinse: Place container, close slider, brew 3× Lungo then 2× Hot Water; empty drip tray.
  • Factory Cup Defaults: Ristretto 25 ml • Espresso 40 ml • Lungo 110 ml • Americano 25 ml coffee + 125 ml water • Hot Water 200 ml.
  • 10s Eject & Top-Up: Capsule auto-ejects after 10 seconds. Press Brew within 10s after brew ends to manually top up (or select Water icon for water top-up).
  • Stop Flow Mid-Brew: Press Brew button or slide capsule slider slightly right (no top-up after manual stop).

⚙️ pp. 13–16 — Settings Mode, Programming & Emptying

  • Enter Settings Mode: Machine OFF + slider closed → press & hold Brew button for 6 seconds (both dials flash) → select beverage icon → press Brew:
  • Dial Menu Map: Ristretto = Descaling • Espresso = Empty System • Lungo = Factory Reset • Americano = Water Hardness • Hot Water = Auto-Off Energy Saving • All Lights = Bluetooth.
  • Exit Settings Mode: Open slider or wait 30s (note: Descaling cannot be exited once confirmed).
  • Auto-Off Energy Saving (p. 13): Temp dial sets 9 min (default), 30 min, 2 hours, or 8 hours; selected icon blinks twice to confirm.
  • Hold-to-Program Limits (p. 14): Ristretto 15–30 ml • Espresso 30–70 ml • Lungo 70–130 ml • Americano 15–110 ml coffee + 25–300 ml water • Hot Water 50–300 ml.
  • Factory Reset (p. 15): Select Lungo + Brew → Temp dial to Max + Brew. Clears pairing, stock counter, and resets hardness to Hard.
  • Emptying System (p. 16): Remove tank first, enter Settings → Espresso + Brew → Temp dial to Max + Brew (steam exits spout; machine stays blocked 10 minutes after emptying).

💧 pp. 17–18 — Daily Cleaning & Water Hardness

  • Cleaning (p. 17): Empty capsule bin daily. Wash tank/lid with odorless detergent and dry. Not dishwasher safe; never immerse machine.

🧼 pp. 19–20 — Official Descaling Procedure (~20 min)

  1. Empty drip tray and used capsule container.
  2. Fill water tank with 2 units Nespresso descaling liquid + potable water up to MAX.
  3. Place a ≥ 1.2 L (41 oz) container under coffee outlet; turn Temp dial to 0 (OFF).
  4. Hold Brew 6s (both dials flash) → select Ristretto + press Brew → Temp dial High "I" + press Brew (Low "O" cancels). Ristretto blinks.
  5. Press Brew to start descaling (coffee & hot water outlets). If paused & Maintenance LED blinks, empty tray/container, replace, and press Brew until tank empties.
  6. Rinse water tank, drip tray, and cup support thoroughly.
  7. Refill tank with 1.2 L (41 oz) fresh potable water; place empty container under outlet.
  8. Press Brew to rinse until tank empties and machine switches OFF automatically. Empty tray. (If Maintenance LED still blinks, < 1.2 L was added — repeat Steps 7–8.)
